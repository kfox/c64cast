- **A face zooming in on a black background gets its own colors as it
  appears.** With `hardware_palette_shots`, a new palette is fitted when the
  picture drifts away from the current one, but the black around a small
  subject counted toward that drift and hid it: a face could stay gray until
  it filled the screen, then turn flesh-colored all at once. Near-black pixels
  no longer count.
